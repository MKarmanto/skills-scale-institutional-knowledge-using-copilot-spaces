# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README is the central entry point for OctoAcme's project management guidance. It summarizes the operating model used across the project lifecycle and links to the supporting process documents in this folder. Use it to quickly orient new team members, understand role expectations, and find the specific workflow guidance needed for initiation, planning, execution, release, and improvement.

OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative delivery model grounded in clear ownership and measurable value. Each initiative is expected to have a defined problem statement, stakeholder list, and success criteria before work begins. Projects move through a repeatable lifecycle—initiation, planning, execution, release, and close—while maintaining strong cross-functional coordination between Product, Project Management, engineering, and QA. The team emphasizes small, testable increments, regular communication, and evidence-based decision-making so priorities remain aligned with business and customer outcomes.

The organization assigns clear roles for delivery and decision-making: Product Managers define outcomes and prioritize the backlog, Project Managers coordinate schedules, risks, and communication, Developers implement and validate the work, QA validates acceptance and quality, and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Communication is structured through weekly alignment, daily standups, sprint reviews, and monthly stakeholder updates, with escalation paths for blockers and risks. Quality is reinforced through acceptance criteria, Definition of Done, CI checks, testing, and post-release verification, ensuring that work is both on track and ready for production.

## Brief summary of OctoAcme project management processes

- Initiation: define the project purpose, scope, stakeholders, and success criteria; establish sponsorship and a clear project brief.
- Planning: create a lightweight plan, backlog, milestones, dependencies, and risk register; assign ownership and estimate effort.
- Execution & Tracking: use short iterations, a shared project board, regular check-ins, and progress reviews to manage delivery and surface blockers.
- Risks & Communication: maintain visibility into risk, dependencies, and stakeholder needs; escalate issues according to the agreed communication path.
- Release & Deployment: verify acceptance criteria, run CI/security checks, complete smoke tests, and coordinate deployment and rollback readiness.
- Retrospective & Continuous Improvement: capture learnings after milestones and releases, document action items, and refine the process over time.
