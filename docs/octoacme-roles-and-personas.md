# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Lead (Operational)

### Role Summary
Delivery Leads own day-to-day execution for a team or workstream, keeping delivery on track and removing blockers across engineering and project work.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate sprint planning, backlog refinement, and day-to-day task assignment
- Remove blockers and escalate cross-team issues
- Report team status, risks, and dependencies to the Program Manager
- Ensure work meets acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done

### How they interact with existing roles
- Works with the Project Manager on schedule, dependencies, and operational health
- Collaborates with Developers on implementation progress and technical constraints
- Coordinates with the QA Lead on readiness and acceptance criteria

### Typical Communication
- Daily delivery check-ins and sprint reviews
- Blocker and dependency updates
- Status reporting to PM and stakeholders

---

## Project Coordinator

### Role Summary
Project Coordinators provide administrative support and continuity across project planning, execution, and follow-up.

### Responsibilities
- Schedule meetings, maintain calendars, and record decisions
- Track action items and follow up on commitments
- Maintain process artifacts, timelines, and runbooks
- Support status reporting and meeting readiness

### How they interact with existing roles
- Supports the Project Manager and Delivery Lead with logistics and tracking
- Helps maintain documentation along with the Knowledge Manager and Technical Writer
- Ensures decisions are visible and assigned to the right owners

### Typical Communication
- Meeting notes and action trackers
- Follow-up reminders and coordination emails
- Status/supporting documentation for team leads

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads own quality strategy and testing readiness so the team can release with confidence.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing approach, coverage, and defect triage workflows
- Maintain test plans and acceptance criteria
- Coordinate regression planning and release readiness checks
- Partner with engineering to verify fixes and quality gates

### How they interact with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers and Delivery Leads on validation and readiness
- Coordinates with the Project Manager on release risk and dependency decisions
- Provides readiness feedback to stakeholders and release owners

### Typical Communication
- Test strategies and quality checkpoints
- Defect triage discussions and release risk updates
- Validation status for sprint and release acceptance

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers oversee delivery readiness and deployment execution for planned releases.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain release checklists, schedules, and runbooks
- Coordinate deployment windows and rollback planning
- Align release activities across engineering, QA, and operations
- Verify release completion and collect post-deployment feedback

### How they interact with existing roles
- Works with Developers and QA Leads to confirm release readiness
- Coordinates with Project Managers on timeline and communication impacts
- Provides updates to stakeholders and leadership as part of rollout readiness

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and readiness reviews
- Deployment notifications and contingency updates
- Post-release status and issue tracking

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons ensure business context is translated into clear delivery priorities and communication.

### Responsibilities
- Gather stakeholder needs and convert them into prioritized asks
- Communicate changes in status, scope, or timing
- Help align expectations with delivery constraints and trade-offs
- Support prioritization workshops and escalation decisions

### How they interact with existing roles
- Works with Product and Project Managers to align scope and timing
- Supports Delivery Leads with business context and priority input
- Collaborates with the Technical Writer or Knowledge Manager for stakeholder-facing updates

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and updates
- Priority and scope clarifications
- Communication plans for project milestones

---

## Knowledge Manager

### Role Summary
Knowledge Managers maintain institutional knowledge and ensure documentation remains accurate and accessible.

### Responsibilities
- Curate and maintain project documentation in the repo and related knowledge repositories
- Own the review and update cadence for process docs and runbooks
- Support onboarding by organizing shared materials and standard operating procedures
- Capture lessons learned and improvements after project milestones

### How they interact with existing roles
- Collaborates with Project Coordinators and Technical Writers to maintain documentation quality
- Works with Delivery Leads to keep runbooks aligned with actual practices
- Helps Project Managers maintain consistent process and governance artifacts

### Typical Communication
- Documentation reviews and update requests
- Onboarding content and process clarifications
- Knowledge-sharing sessions and retrospectives

---

## Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Technical Writers create clear, usable documentation that supports both delivery and communication goals.

### Responsibilities
- Draft and edit process documents, runbooks, and project updates
- Ensure content is clear, consistent, and aligned with templates
- Support documentation quality and readability for internal and external audiences
- Translate technical updates into concise, understandable communication

### How they interact with existing roles
- Works with Knowledge Managers to prioritize updates and documentation quality
- Partners with subject-matter owners such as Delivery Leads, QA Leads, and Release Managers to confirm accuracy
- Supports Stakeholder Liaison communication needs with polished documentation

### Typical Communication
- Documentation reviews and content updates
- Process and runbook revisions
- Stakeholder-facing communication support

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- New personas are intended to clarify ownership, handoff expectations, and escalation paths across project work.

---

## Suggested Role Standards
When adding a persona to future process documentation, include:
- Role summary
- Primary responsibilities
- Decision authority or escalation path
- Interaction points with existing roles
- Key artifacts or documents owned
- Handoffs that require coordination
